### `dependency-parsing`

- [A Claude-assisted first-pass treebank prototype for Quintus (Posthomerica 1.1-30)](treebank_agreement.md) — The three existing parsers already produce full dependency trees that the pipeline discards; capturing them and using their disagreements to scaffold a hand-adjudicated silver CoNLL-U file gets less than half the tokens right by unanimous 3-way agreement, but the disagreements are cheap to spot and the resulting silver file is a real, usable first pass.
